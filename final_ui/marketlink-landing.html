OUR MISSION

Strengthening Local Food Systems

Local farmers markets are growing in popularity as shoppers look for fresh, seasonal, and locally grown produce — but customers rarely know in advance which farmers will be at a market, what stock they have, or at what price.

MarketLink bridges that gap. Farmers publish their inventory and take pre-orders, while customers discover local food and secure their favorites before they sell out.
